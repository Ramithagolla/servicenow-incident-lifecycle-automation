# Phase 5 – Knowledge Integration

## Objective

Integrate the ServiceNow Knowledge Base with the incident lifecycle and attach a relevant troubleshooting article to the incident.

## Incident

**Incident Number:** INC0010014

**Issue:** Unable to connect to company VPN

## Knowledge Article

**Article Number:** KB0010001

**Title:** VPN Connection Troubleshooting

**Knowledge Base:** IT

**Version:** v1.0

**Status:** Approved

## Knowledge Integration

The Knowledge Articles search was used from the incident's Related Search Results. The approved article "VPN Connection Troubleshooting" was located and attached to INC0010014.

## Article Purpose

The article provides troubleshooting guidance for VPN connectivity problems, including checking internet connectivity, restarting the VPN client, verifying credentials, clearing cached credentials, and escalating to the Network team when required.

## Outcome

The relevant approved Knowledge Article was successfully attached to the incident and became part of the incident's resolution evidence.
