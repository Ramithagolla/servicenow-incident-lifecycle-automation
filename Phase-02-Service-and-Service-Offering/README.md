**Classification:** Business Service

**Consumer Type:** Internal

**Parent Service:** IT Incident Management Service

**Short Description:** Standard IT support offering for incident reporting, investigation, escalation and resolution.

The service and service offering provide the foundation for the Incident Lifecycle Automation project.
